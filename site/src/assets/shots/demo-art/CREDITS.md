# Demo library art, credits

Artwork used only as illustrative demo library art in the Quasar documentation screenshots. Each file is a resized (and, for heroes, centre-cropped) copy of a community-uploaded asset from [SteamGridDB](https://www.steamgriddb.com/). Credit to SteamGridDB and to each uploader named below.

The game names, logos and artwork belong to their respective owners (the games' developers and publishers). Their appearance here does not imply any affiliation with or endorsement of Quasar. No image is covered by an open licence.

These files were loaded into the demo stack's app catalog; the screenshots in `site/src/assets/shots/` show them as library tiles and heroes. The picture in the in-session screenshots is a slow pan across `forza-horizon-5-hero.webp`, streamed as video.

Tiles (`*-tile.webp`) are SteamGridDB grids, re-encoded at 600x900. Heroes (`*-hero.webp`) are SteamGridDB heroes, scaled and centre-cropped to 1800x600. Modified: cropped and resized, no other changes.
