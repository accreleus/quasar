## Opening the drawer

**Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q** opens and closes the drawer. **Escape** closes it. Touch
devices get a menu button instead.

## Capturing input

Press **Capture input** in the drawer to lock the pointer to the game. Release
it with the combination shown in the drawer.

### The Escape key

Escape reaches the game only with all three: a Chromium-based browser,
fullscreen, and HTTPS. Otherwise Escape leaves fullscreen, and the drawer says
what's missing. If Escape isn't working, check HTTPS first.

## Fullscreen

Use **Fullscreen** in the session menu. Fullscreen, HTTPS and a Chromium browser
let Escape reach the game rather than leave fullscreen.

## Ending the session

Press **Exit session** in the drawer, or close the tab. Your saves are kept in
your home directory. You then get a short summary of the session.

## Gamepads

Press a button after connecting a controller: browsers hide it until it sends
input. The drawer lists the pads it sees. The game sees a wired Xbox 360
controller.

Your input reaches only your session, not anything else on the host.

## Microphone

If your administrator has enabled it, the bar has a microphone button. It is off
at the start of every session, needs browser permission and HTTPS, and doesn't
respond while input is captured (release capture first).

A live microphone always shows a **MIC** indicator that can't be hidden.

## Quick switch

Swap to another app without ending the session. The stream stays up while the
new app starts.

## Stream settings

| Setting | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| Scaling | Fit the stream to your browser window. |
| Stream resolution | Change the video size, up to your launch size. Return to launch size to resume automatic resolution changes when enabled. |
| Render resolution | Change the game's rendering size, up to the launch size. |
| Interface scale | Adjust the hint used for app interface sizing. |

The launch quality sets the maximum resolution and frame rate. There is no
manual frame-rate control during a session.

## Performance stats

**Performance stats** in the menu shows frame rate, latency, bitrate and jitter
buffer, with a **Detailed** view. The session bar shows frame rate, latency and
bitrate.

- **Frame rate** is what the host produces. Well below your tier means the host
  is the bottleneck, not the network.
- **Presentation interval variation** is how even the frame gaps are. This is
  what stutter feels like.

See [Stream quality problems](/quasar/troubleshooting/quality/).

## Health banners

Quasar shows a banner when the stream is in trouble. It doesn't end or retry
sessions on its own.

| Banner | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Degrading | Quality is dropping. Usually the network. |
| Unsustainable | The stream cannot hold at this level. Consider a lower tier. |
| Not supported on your device | Your browser could not decode what was sent. |
| Reconnecting | The connection dropped and Quasar is trying to recover it. |
