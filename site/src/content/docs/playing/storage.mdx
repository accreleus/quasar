## Where your saves live

You get a persistent home directory per app. Saves, settings and Steam installs
go there and persist between sessions. No two apps or users share one.

It is a real directory on the GPU host, under a root your administrator chooses,
so backing up saves means backing up that directory.

## One session per app at a time

You can't launch an app you already have running; Quasar tells you which session
holds it. Two sessions writing the same saves would corrupt them. Different apps
can run at once, up to your account's session limit.

## Multiple GPU hosts

With shared storage, your files follow you between hosts. With per-host storage,
they don't. Your administrator can set placement to prefer the host that holds
your files.

## Your account pages

Under `/app/account`:

| Page | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Name, email, password. |
| **Stream quality** | Your default quality tier (your administrator may lock it). |
| **Overlay** | What the in-session overlay shows. |
| **Devices** | Browsers you've logged in from and what they can decode. Rename, trust, or revoke (logs that device out). |
| **Sessions** | Your active sessions. End one left running elsewhere. |
| **Storage** | Your home directories and their sizes. |

<Aside type="caution" title="Deleting a home directory deletes the saves in it">
There is no undo. Without backups, deleted means gone.
</Aside>
