## What is in here

You can filter by kind:

- **Games**, launched directly.
- **Desktops**, a full desktop session.
- **Launchers**, a storefront client such as Steam.

Cover art comes from SteamGridDB if your administrator set a key; otherwise
tiles are plain gradients.

## Favourites

The heart on a tile pins it to the top.

## Choosing quality

Pick a quality tier before launch, such as 1080p60 or 1440p120. Twelve tiers
ship, from 720p60 to 4K120. Start with 1080p60.

A tier is a preference. The server picks the best setting that:

1. your GPU host can encode,
2. your browser can decode (measured at login),
3. hasn't failed for you before.

Every tier falls back to H.264, so asking for more than the host can do gets you
the best it can manage, not an error.

<Aside type="note" title="Set it once">
Set a default tier under **Account ▸ Stream quality**. Your administrator may
have set or locked an instance default.
</Aside>

## Why something might not launch

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| No host available | No GPU host is online. Tell your administrator. |
| Capacity exhausted | Hosts are full. Try again later. |
| Session limit reached | You are at your account's session limit. |
| Already running | You already have this app running. One at a time, to protect your saves. |
| Not entitled | You do not have access. Ask your administrator. |

## Steam

If your administrator has enabled the Steam library, installed Steam games
appear as their own tiles. Only installed games appear: install one through the
Steam launcher app and it shows up on the next scan.
