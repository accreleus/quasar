Your library lives at `/app`. It shows the apps you have been granted, and
nothing else. If someone else on the instance has access to something you do
not, you will not see it at all.

## What is in here

Apps come in three kinds and you can filter by them:

- **Games**, launched directly.
- **Desktops**, a full desktop session rather than a single title.
- **Launchers**, which open a storefront client such as Steam and let you pick
  from there.

Artwork comes from SteamGridDB if your administrator has configured a key. If
not, tiles render as gradient panels. It is cosmetic either way.

## Favourites

The heart on a tile pins it. Favourites surface first, which is worth doing if
your catalog is large.

## Choosing quality

Before you launch you can pick a quality tier: a resolution and frame rate pair
such as 1080p60 or 1440p120. Twelve tiers ship by default, from 720p60 up to
4K120, and 1080p60 is the recommended starting point.

What you pick is a **preference**, not a command. Each tier is an ordered list
of encoder settings, and the server takes the first one that works for your
particular situation:

1. Your GPU host has to be able to encode it.
2. Your browser has to be able to decode it. Quasar measures this when you log
   in, so it already knows.
3. If a codec has failed for you before, it gets skipped.

Every tier ends in an H.264 rung, which every browser can decode. That is the
guaranteed floor, so a launch never fails purely because nothing was playable.

The practical consequence is that asking for 4K120 on a machine that cannot
manage it gets you the best rung it can manage, not an error.

<Aside type="note" title="Set it once">
Account, Stream quality lets you set a default tier so you are not choosing
every time. Your administrator may have set an instance default, and may have
locked it.
</Aside>

## Why something might not launch

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| No host available | Nothing in the fleet is online. This is for your administrator. |
| Capacity exhausted | Hosts are up but full. Wait for someone to finish and try again. |
| Session limit reached | You already have as many sessions running as your account allows. |
| Already running | You have a live session of this same app. Only one at a time, because your save files cannot have two writers. |
| Not entitled | You do not have access to this app. Ask your administrator. |

## Steam

If your administrator has enabled the Steam library, games already installed in
the Steam container appear as their own launchable tiles rather than making you
go through the Steam client every time.

This is filesystem discovery of what is installed, not a sync of everything you
own. A game you own but have not installed will not appear. Install it once
through the Steam launcher app and it shows up on the next scan.
