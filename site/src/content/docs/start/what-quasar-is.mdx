---
title: What Quasar is
description: Quasar runs games on GPU servers you own and streams them to a browser.
---

Quasar is a self-hostable cloud gaming platform: GeForce Now, but the servers and
the library are yours. Games run in containers on your GPU machines, and you play
them in a browser tab.

You log in, pick a game and press play. Quasar starts it on a GPU host with room,
encodes it on the GPU, and streams video, audio and input to your browser over
WebRTC. Close the tab and the session ends.

## What you get

- **Many GPU hosts, one library.** Add a GPU machine and it joins the pool.
  Sessions go wherever there is room.
- **Many users.** Each user has their own home directory, saves and app access.
  Registration can be closed, invite-only or open.
- **Hardware encoding** on AMD, NVIDIA (Vulkan Video) and Intel (VA-API).
- **Adaptive bitrate**, on by default and tuned for smoothness.
- **Nothing to install on the client.** Chrome or Chromium on desktop. Keyboard,
  mouse, touch and gamepads work.
- **An admin area** at `/admin` for apps, images, stream profiles, hosts, GPUs,
  sessions and users.

## What Quasar is not

- **Not Moonlight or GameStream.** Moonlight clients can't connect, and never will.
- **Not a game store.** Quasar launches container images; you bring the games.
  Steam is supported as a library provider, so owned Steam titles show up as tiles.
- **Not built to face the internet.** Play over a LAN or VPN. Quasar has not been
  security reviewed. See [Reaching Quasar remotely](/quasar/network/remote-access/).
- **No native client yet.** The browser is the only client.

## Who it suits

It suits homelab owners with an idle GPU who want to play from a laptop, tablet or
TV, and households sharing one GPU.

It suits you less if you want to play over the open internet without a VPN, want a
one-click appliance, or aren't comfortable with Docker and Linux.

## Where Quasar came from

Quasar reuses the Wayland compositor (`gst-wayland-display`) and virtual input
(`inputtino`) from the [Wolf](https://github.com/games-on-whales/wolf) project
(MIT). Wolf speaks Moonlight and grew from a single-host core; Quasar is
multi-host, multi-user and streams to browsers. If you want Moonlight, use Wolf.
