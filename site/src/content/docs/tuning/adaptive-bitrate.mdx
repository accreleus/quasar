---
title: Adaptive bitrate
description: What ABR does, the two modes, when to change mode, and how to turn it off.
---

Adaptive bitrate (ABR) watches round trip time, packet loss and the congestion
controller's bandwidth estimate, and moves the encoder's target bitrate to match.
It is on by default in `smooth` mode. Most deployments should leave it there.

## The two modes

Change it under **Admin ▸ Fleet ▸ Hosts ▸ Settings**. For a source checkout or an
older Compose-managed agent, `QUASAR_ABR_MODE` in `deploy/.env` also sets the host
default. Current managed installs use the host settings page.

| Mode | Behaviour | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `smooth` (default) | Steps down gently: when the encoder is saturated, a non-emergency cut is at most 12.5% per step, 7 seconds apart. Confirmed congestion still triggers a fast drop. | Almost always. |
| `protective` | The older governor. One hard step down on trouble, gradual ramp back up. | `smooth` behaves oddly on your hardware, especially the encoder speed ladder. |
| `off` | Fixed bitrate at the tier's value. The congestion controller stays attached for telemetry only. | A dedicated wired LAN. Not WiFi. |

## Environment override

For an environment-managed agent, `QUASAR_ABR_MODE=off` disables ABR. The older
alias `QUASAR_ABR=0` is also accepted. Prefer the host settings page for a managed
install.

## The floor

ABR never goes below a floor: by default 30% of the tier's bitrate, and at least
500 kbps. Set it explicitly with `QUASAR_ABR_FLOOR_KBPS`, or change the ratio with
`QUASAR_ABR_FLOOR_RATIO`.

## The encoder speed ladder

In `smooth` mode, sustained encoder saturation raises the encoder's speed setting
instead of only cutting bitrate. It unwinds after two healthy windows. Disable it
with `QUASAR_ABR_LADDER=0`.

Resolution and frame-rate rungs are opt-in (default off):

- `QUASAR_ABR_LADDER_RESOLUTION` moves through the session's supported
  external-size ladder. Support depends on the codec and image; Vulkan HEVC can't
  resize live.
- `QUASAR_ABR_LADDER_FPS` halves 120 fps to 60 and restores it. A 60 fps launch
  has no lower rung.

Both need `smooth` mode and apply to the next session.

## Hardware encoders only

ABR is validated on hardware encoders. With openh264 it barely works, because the
software encoder can't saturate 1080p at 8 Mbps, so no congestion is ever seen.
On software encoding, ABR is not why your stream is poor.

NVENC sometimes overshoots the target on busy scenes. That's known behaviour, not
a misconfiguration.

## Watching it work

**Admin ▸ Sessions**, session detail, plots the ABR setpoint against the congestion
controller's estimate. A rapidly sawtoothing setpoint means something is fighting
it: check loss and round trip time in the same window first.

## Fine tuning

About fifteen more settings control the governor's hysteresis (smoothing,
deadband, step sizes, dwell times, cliff guards). The defaults are the tested
behaviour. Don't change them without a measurement to compare against
([Diagnostics](/quasar/operations/diagnostics/)). The full list is in
[Environment variables](/quasar/reference/environment/).
